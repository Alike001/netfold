# Previous Open House Winners and Judging Signals

## Open House NYC

Arbitrum Foundation reported 512 builders from more than 40 countries.

Winners:

1. Tilt Protocol
2. Fangorn
3. EqualFi

The Foundation says evaluation considered technical execution, product clarity, ecosystem alignment and long-term potential. It also said the winning teams showed clear user focus, promising architecture, and a concise roadmap beyond the buildathon.

This matters more than copying any winner's sector.

## Open House London

Arbitrum Foundation reported 782 builders and 278 project submissions across DeFi, AI, RWAs, payments, privacy and other categories.

Public winner coverage and project descriptions show strong finance/product depth. Examples from London include products around trading aggregation, DEX infrastructure, perpetuals, privacy/stablecoins and agentic finance.

## Signal for our submission

A judge should be able to understand these points in roughly 30 seconds:

- Who has the problem?
- What is painful today?
- Why is an onchain shared state useful?
- What exactly does the contract enforce?
- Where is the live Arbitrum transaction evidence?
- What is intentionally out of scope?

A compact, finished product can score better than a large but weakly evidenced architecture.
